# Unit Economics & Revenue Projection

## 1. Value of a Converted Lead
A "Lead" is a startup spending >$2,000/mo on AI. 
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** If Credex saves them 30% through credits, and Credex takes a small margin, a single enterprise client can be worth **$5,000 - $10,000 ARR** to Credex.
- **Conversion Math:** 100 Audits -> 10 High-Savings Leads -> 2 Consultations -> 1 Closed Deal.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Since we are using organic channels (GTM Plan), our initial CAC is purely "Time Cost."
- **Estimated CAC:** ~$50 per lead (calculated by the time spent in outreach/content creation).

## 3. Profitability Threshold
To be profitable, we need an "Audit to Consultation" conversion rate of **5%**.
- **The Goal:** 500 audits per month = 25 consultations = 5 new clients = ~$30k ARR growth/month.

## 4. The Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M ARR, the tool must become the "Industry Standard Benchmark."
- **Strategy:** Launch an "Embeddable Widget" (Bonus Feature) that SaaS review sites can use.
- **Viral Loop:** When one founder shares a "I saved $600" report, 3 other founders in their network will audit their stack.
