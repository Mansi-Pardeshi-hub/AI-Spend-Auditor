# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy - AI Spend Auditor

## 1. Target User Persona
Our "Ideal Customer Profile" (ICP) isn't just "startups." It is specifically:
- **The Target:** Head of Engineering or Lead DevOps at Series A/B startups (team size 20-50).
- **The Pain Point:** They have 20+ developers using Cursor/Copilot and several API keys in production, but no centralized way to track "tier-bloat" or credit eligibility.
- **The Moment of Intent:** They just saw a $5,000+ monthly bill from OpenAI or Anthropic and were asked by the CFO to "look into it."

## 2. Getting the First 100 Users ($0 Budget)
We will avoid generic SEO and focus on "High-Trust Niche Channels":
1. **The "Engineering Manager" DM Strategy:** Cold DM 50 Engineering Managers on X/LinkedIn who have recently posted about "AI productivity." Offer a free "SaaS Spend Snapshot" using our tool.
2. **Niche Slack/Discord Communities:** Post a "Launch Discount" or "Audit Alpha" in communities like *Lark*, *Y Combinator Founder Network*, and *Indie Hackers*.
3. **Platform-Specific "Leakage" Posts:** Write a detailed technical post on Hacker News/Reddit about "How we saved $400/mo by switching from Cursor Business to Pro." Link the tool as the "calculator" for others to do the same.

## 3. The Unfair Distribution Channel
**Credex Partnership:** Since this tool is a lead-gen asset for Credex, we can leverage Credex’s existing database of companies that previously inquired about credits but didn't convert. We offer them the Audit as a "Health Checkup" to re-engage them.

## 4. Week-1 Traction Metrics
- **Audits Completed:** 150+
- **Lead Capture Rate:** >30% (Email submissions for the report)
- **Viral Shares:** 10+ unique public URLs shared on X/LinkedIn.
